HOB'S VOICE

Old Hob's babble in Bandit Math: no words, a little syllable a word, each at its own pitch. Move a slider and press Say something to hear it. When it sounds right, Copy for the game and paste the block over the one at the top of bandit-math/src/audio/voice.js. (The syllable itself is the lab's 'VOICE — OLD HOB' patch.)
